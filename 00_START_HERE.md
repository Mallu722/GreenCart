## 🎯 The Quick Summary

GreenCart is a **Blinkit-style plant delivery app** where:
- Users browse and filter plants/seeds
- Add items to cart
- Complete checkout (2 steps)
- Get 45-minute delivery
- Track orders
- Admin can manage products

Everything is **working and ready to use** right now.
